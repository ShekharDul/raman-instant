Instant Raman Guide

Protocol & Technical Specifications

01. About the App

Instant Raman is a fast, simple tool built to help researchers process Raman spectroscopy data. It handles the heavy lifting of baseline correction and data visualization right in your browser, so you can focus on your results.

02. Baseline Correction

We use a method called the SNIP algorithm (Statistics-sensitive Non-linear Iterative Peak-clipping) to clean up your spectral data.

How it works

Instead of using complicated curves that might accidentally hide your real data, the SNIP method carefully "clips" away the background noise (like fluorescence) while keeping your Raman peaks intact.

Iterations: You can use the slider in the sidebar to adjust how much background to remove. More iterations generally mean a smoother, deeper baseline removal.

03. Preparing Your Data

To get the best results, make sure your files follow these simple rules:

Supported Formats

  • Universal Formats: .dx, .jdx (JCAMP-DX)
  • Native Exports: .dpt (Bruker), .xml / .txt (Horiba LabSpec)
  • Generic Data: .csv, .txt, .asc
  • Standard UTF-8 encoding.

Layout

For generic text files, the app expects two columns (Shift and Intensity). For professional formats (DX, Bruker, Horiba), the app automatically handles complex headers, metadata, and non-linear spacing.

04. How to Use

Step 1: Upload

Simply click the upload box or drag your files onto the screen. You can upload multiple files at once to compare them.

Step 2: Adjust

  • Auto Mode: Use the slider to let the app automatically find the baseline.
  • Manual Mode: Click directly on the graph to place your own baseline points if you need more control.

Step 3: Compare & View

Use the Layout menu to switch between seeing a single plot, stacking them (Waterfall), or viewing them in a grid.

05. Saving Your Work

When you’re happy with the results, you can download your data in several ways:

  • Analytical Excel: A clean spreadsheet containing your raw data, the calculated baseline, and the final corrected data.
  • Publication Image: High-quality PNG or Vector SVG graphs, ready for papers.
  • Interactive Report: A self-contained "HTML Capsule" that you can email to collaborators. It includes the interactive plot, all peak data, and your full processing history in one file.

06. Cosmic Ray Rejection

Sensitive CCD detectors used in Raman spectrometers are susceptible to high-energy cosmic rays, which appear as sharp, unphysical spikes in your data. Instant Raman implements a non-destructive Z-score sliding window algorithm using Median Absolute Deviation (MAD).

The Healing Process: When a spike is detected, the engine "heals" the data using a local median filter, ensuring your integration and peak areas remain mathematically accurate for quantitative work.

07. Advanced Spectral Deconvolution

For complex research, Instant Raman features a professional Levenberg-Marquardt (LM) non-linear fitting engine. This allows you to resolve overlapping bands that are otherwise impossible to quantify.

Supported Profiles

  • Lorentzian: Standard for crystalline materials (e.g., Silicon, Diamond).
  • Gaussian: Optimized for disordered materials or glasses.
  • Voigt (4-Param): The industry gold standard, combining Lorentzian and Gaussian profiles for maximum accuracy.

Professional Visualization

The deconvolution view provides a stacked vertical layout with a zero-centered residual plot. This allows you to visually verify the "goodness-of-fit" and detect hidden spectral features through residual analysis.

08. Peak Metrology

Center positions are calculated using parabolic interpolation of the three highest points around each peak maximum. This provides center positions with higher precision than the raw data spacing. FWHM is calculated directly from the data by identifying the half-maximum intensity threshold on both sides of the peak. For overlapping bands, the LM deconvolution engine should be used for rigorous results.
